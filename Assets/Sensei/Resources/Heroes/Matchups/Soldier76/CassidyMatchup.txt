<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You and Cassidy deal roughly the same damage. If fighting at a distance, you can use Biotic Field to keep yourself sustained in a longer fight. while evading shots. Each shot from Cassidy will deal substantial damage if they hit, though, so try to focus on evasion if possible. Be careful not to be caught up close by Cassidy, who can use Flashbang and Fan the Hammer to quickly kill you. If possible, try to obtain the high ground to fire at Cassidy's head from above.
