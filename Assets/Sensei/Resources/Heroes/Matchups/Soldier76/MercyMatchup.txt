<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If Mercy is flying about in the air, she'll prove an easy target for you to pin down at a distance. Otherwise, she may be hiding behind teammates or cover, in which case let another teammate try to deal with her unless she emerges from cover, in which case you can try to quickly gun her down. If her team isn't nearby and she's attempting to flee, you can use Sprint to chase her down and finish her off before she can get away.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
If your ultimate is ready, communicate with the Mercy on your team, as a boost from her Caduceus Staff can make the perfect aim of Tactical Visor even more potent.
