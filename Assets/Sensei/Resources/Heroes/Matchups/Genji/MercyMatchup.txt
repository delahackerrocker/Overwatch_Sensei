<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Mercy has very low mobility unless she has a teammate to use Guardian Angel on. You are best suited to chase and kill Mercy, especially seeing how she doesn't have any abilities to disrupt your attacks.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Your high mobility can provide your Mercy with a strong getaway option. If a fight is turning sour, quickly retreat and stay in your Mercy's field of view, allowing her to use Guardian Angel to keep up with you and help her escape.
