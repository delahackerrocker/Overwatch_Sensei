<b>MATCH-UP</b>
As a sniper, Ashe usually prefers to stay in the back. You can easily sneak up on her and flank her so long as you stay out of her line of sight. Even at a distance, you can Deflect her scoped shots. You can also deflect her Coach Gun pellet to damage her as she tries to flee. With Dynamite, you can deflect it but your Shurikens won't detonate it earlier. Also, you can burn yourself with Dynamite (since Ashe normally could). With B.O.B., you can't block his charge but you can deflect the bullets. It won't be enough to kill B.O.B. since Deflect has a short duration. However, you can redirect those bullets to Ashe or another enemy player.
