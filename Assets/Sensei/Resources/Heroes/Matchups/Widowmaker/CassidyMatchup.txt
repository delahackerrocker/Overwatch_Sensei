<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While Cassidy can fire powerful rounds at long range, he's nowhere near as powerful and accurate as you are with your Widow's Kiss. Keep an eye out for him and you can take him out with one headshot or a couple of bodyshots. If you're worried about him sneaking up on you and hitting you with a Flashbang, place your Venom Mine near a flanking location; not only will this deal some damage to him, it will also alert you of his presence so you can unscope and prepare to fight or flee with your Grappling Hook.
