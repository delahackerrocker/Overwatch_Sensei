<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Since Brigitte has no vertical mobility, playing on the high ground will keep you safe from her powerful damage up close. If you catch her out in the open, she's easy prey. Keep in mind, her Barrier has enough health to block 3 shots before breaking, so she can buy time to get to cover. Like with other targets with Barriers, look to take off angles and snipe her from behind where she can't shield herself.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Use armour from her Rally to essentially gain extra HP, which is always valuable on Widowmaker.
