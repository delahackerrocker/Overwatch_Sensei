<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Torbjörn's turret is stationary and takes 3 shots to destroy. Stay out of its range and snipe it down for your team. Against Torbjörn himself, it's fairly easy to headshot him, just don't get too confident: a good Torbjörn can outsnipe you because his primary fire has no damage falloff. Also you can try to force his Overload by bodyshotting him from a distance, so he won't have it to fight your team.
