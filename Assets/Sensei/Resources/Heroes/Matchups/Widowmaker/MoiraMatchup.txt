<b>MATCH-UP</b>
There's not anything Moira can do to you as long as you keep your distance, she is a vulnerable target and a top priority to kill. However if she gets close enough she can use her damage orb and biotic grasp to quickly take you out. Don't be afraid to stand still and perfect your aim, as her grasp doesn't require aiming to use properly.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
There is no notable team synergy with Moira.
