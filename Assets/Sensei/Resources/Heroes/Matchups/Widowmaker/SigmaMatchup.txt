<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Due to his limited range, Sigma can't do much to you if you're sniping him from afar. However his Barrier can force you to reposition if he places it right in front of your face. You can force Sigma to waste abilities on you by harassing him as often as possible while keeping a safe distance so he can't damage you back, and also shoot him out of the sky during his Ultimate if he doesn't play behind cover.
