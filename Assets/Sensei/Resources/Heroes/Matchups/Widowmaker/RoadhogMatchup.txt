<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Roadhog's limited range means he can't do much against you if you keep your distance, however his high health and survivability means there's not a lot you can do to him either. If he flanks and successfully lands a hook, you're doomed. So keep tabs on him and try not to let him out of your sight. Go for headshots after he lands a hook or is using his Ultimate for some easy damage and a chance to force his Take a Breather.
