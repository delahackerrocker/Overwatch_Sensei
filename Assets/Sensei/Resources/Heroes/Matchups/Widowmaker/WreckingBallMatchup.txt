<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Wrecking Ball is a large threat, as he can quickly close the distance on you, overpower you in close range combat and escape. A lone Widowmaker is the perfect target for him to bully, so keep tabs on him and keep your Grappling Hook available at all times to escape as soon as he commits onto you. Sometimes, this won't work as he can easily keep up and finish you off, so try to stick with your powerful close-range allies like Roadhog or Brigitte so he can't commit onto you without risking his own life.
