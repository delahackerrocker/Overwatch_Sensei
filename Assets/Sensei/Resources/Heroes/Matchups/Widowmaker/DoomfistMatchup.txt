<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Doomfist is another threat you need to watch out for, as if you're not careful he can quickly close the distance, kill you, and leave before you or your team can stop him. Even with your automatic fire he will have no trouble overpowering you up close, so it's usually better to flight over fight. If he's not going for you however, he's easy to kill or harass due to his large hitbox.
