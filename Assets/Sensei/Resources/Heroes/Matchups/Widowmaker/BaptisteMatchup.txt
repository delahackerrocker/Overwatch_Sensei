<b>MATCH-UP</b>
The Immortality Field can prevent you from taking out Baptiste's teammates, so be wary of it or take it out as quickly as you can. If he ever uses Exo Boots in the open, he's a free kill due to his slow and predictable movement, so use the opportunity to remove him from the fight.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Through the use of Amplification Matrix, Widowmaker can one shot every hero in the game (except D.VA and Wrecking Ball due to their armour) so try to get a Baptiste to place his matrix in your sniper's nest.
