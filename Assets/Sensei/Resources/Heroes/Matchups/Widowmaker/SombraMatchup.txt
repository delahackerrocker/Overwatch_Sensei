<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Without the use of Infra-Sight, you have little way of detecting Sombra as she uses her Thermoptic Camo and Translocator to approach and harass you. While Sombra's Machine Pistol doesn't have massive killing potential, she can still usually outdamage you in a close-range fight. Even if you get Sombra down to low health, her Translocator will allow her to retreat back to her team and heal, while you'll have to either stay in the open at low health or fall back and heal. If Sombra trips on your Venom Mine, she'll lose her stealth while also becoming visible through walls, making her an easy target. During Infra-Sight, keep a sharp eye out for Sombra and try to use this opportunity to eliminate her.
