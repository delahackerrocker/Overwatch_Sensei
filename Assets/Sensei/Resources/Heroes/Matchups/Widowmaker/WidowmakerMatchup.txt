<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Ironically, an enemy Widowmaker is your single biggest threat because she can take you out with a single headshot. Generally speaking Widowmaker vs Widowmaker comes down to raw aim and who lands the headshot first, however you can make the duel easier for yourself by killing her while she's scoped in and focusing on your teammates, as she'll be vulnerable and unable to react in time.
