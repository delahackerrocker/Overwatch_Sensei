<b>MATCH-UP</b>
With her Barrier and Fortify, Orisa has strong tools to relieve herself from your shots. There's not a lot Widowmaker can to do Orisa as she'll playing around her Barrier very often, and it can be difficult to flank her as she'll usually be sticking with her team. Unless you can catch her in the open without her Barrier or Fortify, try to prioritise other targets.
