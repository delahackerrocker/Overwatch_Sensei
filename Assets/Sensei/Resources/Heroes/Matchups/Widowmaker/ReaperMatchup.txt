<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Reaper's strength comes from close-range combat, which you will rarely find yourself in. Keep an eye on him at a distance, and you should have no problem headshotting him. He may use Shadow Step to quickly approach you and shoot you down before you can react. Be aware of the noise of a nearby Shadow Step; if you hear it, quickly unscope and try to pre-place your crosshair where his head will appear for an easy kill. You do need to time it accurately or he can use Wraith Form to dodge it.
