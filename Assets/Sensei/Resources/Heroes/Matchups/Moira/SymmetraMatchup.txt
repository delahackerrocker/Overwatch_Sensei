<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Moira is fairly effective against Symmetra because you can easily destroy her sentry turrets and you can typically beat her in a 1v1 unless her beam is fully charged or she lands multiple secondary fire orbs. A good reference to know is that Moira's Biotic Grasp has about double the range of Symmetra's beam so you can force her to rely on her secondary fire orbs which are easy to dodge at a distance.
