<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Never 1v1 Roadhog unless his time is more valuable than yours. You simply can't kill him on your own because of his one shot potential and self healing. Getting hooked is a death sentence, but if you do get hooked, hold the Fade key so that it instantly activates if you manage to survive. His Hook has the same range as your Biotic Grasp, (20 metres) so use this to space yourself accordingly and avoid it.
