<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While in her mech, D.Va is strong against Moira because she can destroy your Biotic Orbs with Defense Matrix and in a 1v1 scenario, kill you quite quickly with her high burst damage. Since your Biotic Grasp damage is negligible against her high HP and armour, focus on using Fade to run away if she tries rushing you; for maximum distance, use Fade at the end of her Boosters ability. Interestingly, if D.Va loses her mech, she can suddenly become very vulnerable to Moira because she only has a measly 150 HP and only her pistol to defend herself with. For this reason, if she uses Self Destruct, you can rush her with a damage orb and focus Biotic Grasp on her to kill her before she gets another mech, and then use Fade to survive the blast.
