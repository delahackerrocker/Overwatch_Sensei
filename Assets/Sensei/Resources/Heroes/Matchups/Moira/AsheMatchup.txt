<b>MATCH-UP</b>
At range, Ashe can make quick work of you with her impressive rifle, so avoid open sightlines she may be holding and try to flank. Most Ashe players panic when they are flanked and instantly use Coach Gun to create separation, however Moira can easily dodge the knockback and catch up with the Ashe through the use of Fade. Once you close the distance she will have no means to escape you, so finish her off with a damage orb/biotic grasp combo.
