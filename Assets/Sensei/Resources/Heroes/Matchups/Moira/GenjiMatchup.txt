<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Surprisingly, Moira is incredibly effective against Genji. You can use Fade to dodge his Swift Strike and damage him through Deflect with Biotic Orb, Biotic Grasp or Coalescence, all while being out of his effective range. In a 1v1 you can also outheal most of his damage with a healing orb as long as he's not point blank distance and bursting you down with secondary fires. You can also prevent Genji from getting a dash reset in the midst of Dragonblade by using Fade to create distance if he dashes into you. Additionally, your Coalescence healing can completely negate his Dragonblade swing damage, unless he is nanoboosted.
