<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Soldier is a very difficult matchup for Moira, his Biotic Field can negate most of your damage and he can easily burst you down with his rifle and Helix Rockets. If he has his Biotic Field up, Soldier should be avoided at all costs, but if he doesn't and you know this, you can try to 1v1 and save Fade to dodge Helix Rockets, but if he has good aim, prioritise other targets as you'll lose most of the time. Also, your Coalescence self-healing is outdamaged by his Tactical Visor quite significantly, so just use a Fade to break line of sight rather than trying to challenge him with your own ultimate.
