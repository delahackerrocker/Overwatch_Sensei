<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You can't keep up with Wrecking Ball, so don't waste your time trying to go for him. All you can really do is support allies he is diving, as you can typically outheal the follow-up damage from his Piledrive. If he ever dives you, a simple Fade may not always be enough to escape him. Try to do a Fade jump off of a small object to high ground so has to use multiple cooldowns to catch up, giving you more time to retreat to your team.
