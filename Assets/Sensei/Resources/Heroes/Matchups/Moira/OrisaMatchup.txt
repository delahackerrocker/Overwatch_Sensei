<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Orisa is a poor target for Moira to challenge to a 1v1, because she has a lot of armour and defensive abilities to lower your damage on her, which is already quite low. Don't waste your time trying to break her Barrier or contribute damage to her unless they are low on health, and instead focus on supporting your allies here. Additionally, since Orisa's usually defend their Supercharger with their Barrier, you can use Coalescence to penetrate it and destroy the Supercharger and save your team from a lot of stress.
