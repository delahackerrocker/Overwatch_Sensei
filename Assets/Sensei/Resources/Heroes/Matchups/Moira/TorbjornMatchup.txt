<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Torbjörn is another very difficult match-up for Moira, he just has too much armour and burst damage to the point that you lose every time unless he misses most of his shots and/or doesn't use his turret. Destroying the turret is not your job at all, however if you must you can use a healing orb to heal yourself while you break it with Biotic Grasp; keep in mind, the turret is not affected by damage orbs. Don't waste your time trying to deal with Torbjörn and leave this one to another teammate.
