<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Pharah's main role is aerial control, so a fight between two Pharahs is an intense and long-lasting dogfight that ends when one or the other runs out of fuel. The primary fire is hard to hit as it travels very slowly. Try to predict the other Pharah's movements and make a clear shot for your rocket to hit. Only 2 shots without healing is enough to kill Pharah mid-air, but bear in mind that this applies to you as well.
