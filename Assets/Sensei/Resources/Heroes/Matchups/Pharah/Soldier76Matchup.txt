<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Soldier: 76 has far better range than Cassidy, so it isn't hard for him to shoot you down mid-air. With a larger magazine, perfect accuracy isn't as necessary as with Cassidy. He can keep you at bay, killing you mid-air or applying enough pressure to force you to stay on ground behind cover. If the enemy's Soldier: 76 has a good enough aim to bring you down while flying, try to get some cover and shoot a rocket to disrupt his position. On the ground, Pharah is putting herself at risk to be hit by Helix Rockets, which travel at a faster speed. Tactical Visor is a great counter during your flight, which brings you to ground as a corpse within seconds. If the enemy team has a Soldier: 76, it's best to stay on the ground or focus him before he can shoot you down.
