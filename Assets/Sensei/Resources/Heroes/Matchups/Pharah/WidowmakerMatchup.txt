<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Widowmaker's nature as a hitscan hero with unlimited range and no damage falloff makes her one of the most dangerous for you to face. A fully charged body shot on you is enough for you to lose over half of your health, and a headshot will instantly kill you, though that is fairly difficult to land in many cases. You must avoid allowing Widowmaker to predict your movements; you're better off peeking her from behind cover and shooting at her sniper nest. However, Widowmaker can escape from the spot fairly easy with her Grappling Hook, and if Widowmaker repositions herself frequently, it can be difficult for you to fight back. Using Barrage while an enemy Widowmaker is nearby is risky, as you are immobile while using it, making you an easy target for a headshot.
