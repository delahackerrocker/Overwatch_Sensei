<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Moira will pose no threat to you at long-range, however be wary of flying too close to the ground, as she will be able to use Biotic Grasp on you with its surprisingly long lock-on distance. However, you should still be able to kill her with two direct rockets even if she is attacking you. Coalescence can also pose a risk to you, especially as it has longer range than her primary attack, and she heals constantly during it.
