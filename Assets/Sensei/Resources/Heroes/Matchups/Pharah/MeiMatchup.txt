<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While Mei's Icicle is as fast as Hanzo's arrow, her damage falloff and casting delay cut her from countering Pharah as much as Hanzo does. Mei's stronghold is on the ground where she can trap the enemy, split their team and kill them later. If you remain in the air, Mei's Endothermic Blaster has no way to reach you. With aerial sight over the battlefield, you can warn your teammates about Mei's location and be ready to ruin any of her trap plans. Mei is still capable of fighting Pharah if she manages to land a few Icicles within her effective range, but unlike Hanzo, she needs to deal several shots to kill Pharah.
