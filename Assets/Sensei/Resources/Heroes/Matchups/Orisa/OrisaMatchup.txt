<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Since Orisa is an anchor tank, both of you will be playing with your respective team, which will come down to who's team can manage better, if you find an enemy Orisa wondering about without her team for whatever reasons, the fight will usually last long due to your relatively big health and armor, all of Orisa's abilities are more defensive than offensive and there'll be almost no way to gain the upper hand, if both of you stumble to one another you should backpedal to your team while retaliating to the enemy Orisa rather than trying to stand your ground so you don't waste precious time.
