<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Having a big hitbox and lacking mobility, an encounter with an enemy junkrat is very unfortunate, Your barriers won’t last long against his grenades, your fortify will do very little difference should the enemy junkrat focus on you, and he can save himself from enviormental hazards with his concussion mines, it’s also way easier for him to retreat a fight should it not go well for him, try not to face a junkrat head on and keep a fair distance to make it harder for him to hit you consistently.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You can use Halt! to pull enemies into Junkrat's Concussion Mine.
