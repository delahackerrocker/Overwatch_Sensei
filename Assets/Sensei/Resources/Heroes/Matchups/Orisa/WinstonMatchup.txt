<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Winston is not made to confront tanks, and you're no exception, he has no means of attacking you at a distance, and at close range, his low damage output will only tickle you, however you usually won't be able to kill Winston by yourself before he can disengage with his jump pack and you being able to use halt! to stop him (unless you can predict him about to flee, in which case he'll often be in trouble), if he uses his ultimate you can use fortify to negate the knockback of his punches and shoot at his giant hitbox to gain ultimate charge.
