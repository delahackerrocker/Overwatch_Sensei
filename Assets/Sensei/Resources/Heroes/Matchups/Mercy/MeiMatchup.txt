<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Never wander out alone when Mei is about; should you fail to flee with Ability-mercy3.png Guardian Angel, you'll prove to be an easy target for Mei to pick off, especially if she blocks your escape with Ability-mei3.png Ice Wall. Try to keep Ability-mercy3.png Guardian Angel on standby when you know that Mei is chasing you, as her freezing effect won't slow Ability-mercy3.png Guardian Angel's movement speed. Your healing can easily outheal Mei's Ability-mei1.png Endothermic Blaster's freezing, and if you keep your patient at full health, even once frozen it will be difficult for Mei to finish them off with an icicle headshot.
