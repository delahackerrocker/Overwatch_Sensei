<b>MATCH-UP</b>
It's extremely rare to end up facing another Mercy on the battlefield. While it may be tempting to chase after an enemy Mercy yourself, let your teammates focus on her while you provide a damage boost so they can quickly kill her. If you are caught in a situation where neither of you has teammates nearby, fight back with your Ability-mercy2.png Caduceus Blaster; unless she's near-death, though, focus less on finishing her off and more on backpedaling to your team and regrouping. If you have Ability-mercy6.png Valkyrie active and she doesn't, she's an incredibly easy target to gun down before returning to your team.
