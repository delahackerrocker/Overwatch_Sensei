<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Zenyatta is a long-ranged character for whom Mercy's mobility matters little. His Ability-zenyatta3.png Orb of Discord will make you even more frail than usual, and if applied to your teammate will render it more difficult to outheal incoming damage. Avoid exposing yourself to Zenyatta's attacks, and if he's out in the open, damage boost a teammate so they can quickly kill him.
