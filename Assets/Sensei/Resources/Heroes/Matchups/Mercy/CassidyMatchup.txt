<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Cassidy can easily dispatch you with a few shots from long range, and your ability to fly around to your allies is less useful against ranged characters like Cassidy, as the relative movement isn't as large and can be predictable. In close quarters, Cassidy can stun you with his Ability-mccree3.png Flashbang and then shoot you to death. If Cassidy uses Ability-mccree4.png Deadeye, duck to cover; if you have to abandon an ally you're healing to do so then do it, because otherwise you're both going to die.
