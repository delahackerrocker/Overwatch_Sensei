<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If you're lazily gliding in the air between teammates, you'll serve as an easy target for an enemy Hanzo to snipe at a distance. His quiet nature can make it hard to pinpoint his position, so always be careful before leaving cover. Also be wary of when he's used Ability-hanzo2.png Sonic Arrow, as once he knows where you are, he'll make you a priority target should you peek your head out from cover.
