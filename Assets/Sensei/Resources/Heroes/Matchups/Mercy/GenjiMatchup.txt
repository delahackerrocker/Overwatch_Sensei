<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Genji will usually try to get behind enemy lines and quickly kill you, slipping anyway before anyone can catch him. Genji's Ability-genji3.png Swift Strike is his only means of rapid movement; your Ability-mercy3.png Guardian Angel has a far shorter cooldown, meaning if you have allies nearby, you should usually be able to bail from a fight with Genji. If Genji approaches you while you have an ally nearby, use them as cover and heal them while they fight Genji; you should easily win most two-on-one fights against him.
