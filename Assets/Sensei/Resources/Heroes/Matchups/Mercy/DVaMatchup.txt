<b>MATCH-UP</b>
D.Va can hunt you down with her Ability-dva2.png Boosters, diving into the back line to chase after you. Take care not to be isolated by her, as you don't stand a chance in a one-on-one battle with her; always stick to allies that you can fly between, and let them serve as a meat shield to protect you.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Damage boosting D.Va while she's out of her mech can help her get it back faster. Beyond that, there's almost always a better use of your time.
