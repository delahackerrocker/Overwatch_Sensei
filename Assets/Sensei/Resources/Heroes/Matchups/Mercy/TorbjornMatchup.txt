<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Since Torbjörn's Ability-torbjorn3.png Turret will target the first enemy it sees, let your patient lead the charge to destroy it while you hide behind and heal them. While it may be tempting to use your Ability-mercy2.png Caduceus Blaster to destroy the Ability-torbjorn3.png Turret, you'll sustain considerable damage in the process, making you an easy target for Torbjörn if he shows up to avenge his Ability-torbjorn3.png Turret. Torbjörn himself is very dangerous if you run into him in close quarters, especially when he has Ability-torbjorn4.png Overload active. Keep your distance from him as much as possible.
