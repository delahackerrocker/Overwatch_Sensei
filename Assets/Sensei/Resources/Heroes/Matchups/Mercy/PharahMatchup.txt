<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Pharah's long range allows her to dispatch you from a distance, and makes it easy for her to harass you even when you have a tank protecting you. Be careful when hiding in buildings, as Pharah can launch missiles in after you and you won't be able to escape or retaliate. While your blaster won't deal much damage against Pharah, if she is at low health it might be worth trying to shoot her down.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Among the most famous pairs to Mercy, Pharah's ability to fly coupled with her strong damage output make her a stellar hero to "pocket." However, remember that Pharah has her own vulnerabilities (to heroes such as Widowmaker and Cassidy), which you expose yourself to by staying with her.
