<b>MATCH-UP</b>
A smart Cassidy is capable of angling his Flashbang so that it arcs over your Barrier and hit you, causing you to drop your shield. However, Fan the Hammer is less effective against your due to your high amount of armor, and you can smack Cassidy about with your hammer should the latter be alone. Keep your barrier up if Cassidy is trying to shoot you at a long range.
