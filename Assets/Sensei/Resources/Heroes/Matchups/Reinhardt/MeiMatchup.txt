<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Mei deals little damage to both the barrier and Reinhardt (who is capable of absorbing multiple headshots from her, even while frozen), and her Ice Wall often just gives Reinhardt the opportunity to lower his barrier to recharge it. Be careful about being separated from your team by Ice Wall as it allows you along with your team to be picked off. Also, hitting Mei in Ice Block form stops Reinhardt's charge. If Mei is activating Blizzard, a quick Charge away from her may be your best bet to avoid being frozen.
