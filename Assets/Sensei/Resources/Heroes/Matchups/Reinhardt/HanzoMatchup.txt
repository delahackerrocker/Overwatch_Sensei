<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Hanzo's arrows will be stopped cold by Reinhardt's barrier, but enough pressure from his Storm Arrows could bring your barrier close to breaking, so be careful. His Sonic Arrow will warn his allies about any pushes you and your team may be attempting, as teammates often tend to gather behind Reinhardt for protection. Your barrier will provide no protection from Dragonstrike; if you hear Hanzo activating it, drop your barrier and move out of the way.
