<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your barrier will allow you to soak up a lot of damage from Soldier: 76's standard attacks, and it is capable of completely stopping his Tactical Visor cold. However, Soldier: 76 still stands a chance against you in close-long range skirmishes as his damage is far greater than most offense heroes. You have no means with which to attack Soldier: 76 at long-range; stick with your team when he's around, or if necessary try to bait him into a close-quarters fight where you can smash him with your hammer.
