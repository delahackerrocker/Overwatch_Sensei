<b>MATCH-UP</b>


<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Moira serves as a support who is, at the same time, an offensive threat. By staying near a friendly Moira's side to protect her, you have not only a healer to bail you out, but a powerful and mobile damager as well. Her ultimate, Coalescence, may not be the most game-changing, but can offer great value when used smartly. Stay in Moira's beam when she uses it, so that you can be healed while foes are being damaged.
