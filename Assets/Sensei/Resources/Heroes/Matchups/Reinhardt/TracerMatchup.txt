<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Tracer can blink behind your barrier and rapidly unload her pistols into your large hitbox. Pulse Bomb can bring you down to 105 health, leaving you in a critical condition. 2 blows from the Rocket Hammer is enough to kill Tracer if she does not have Recall, consider doing so when needed, otherwise stick with your team and try to be ready to change direction to block her flanking attacks.
