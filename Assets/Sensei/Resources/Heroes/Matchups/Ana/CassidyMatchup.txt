<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Cassidy has solid range, very high accuracy, and high damage, making it relatively easy for him to pick you off at range. Likewise, his Flashbang can incapacitate you in close quarters. However, his lack of mobility makes him a relatively easy target for you to hit, and at longer distances you'll be able to out-damage him. Sleep Dart can shut him down, especially if you hit him while he's preparing Deadeye. Keep your distance, and only approach tighter spaces with your team so you aren't ambushed.
