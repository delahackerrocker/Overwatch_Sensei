<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Orisa's Fusion Driver is excellent at laying down suppressive fire at your location to keep you from getting clean shots. In addition, her Halt can pull you from high ground and out of cover, putting you in a vulnerable position. Her Protective Barrier will block your abilities, however it cannot be moved once placed until the cooldown is over. You can take advantage of this and wait for her shield to be on cooldown before using abilities like Biotic Grenade. Fortify will render her completely immune to your Sleep Dart, so wait for her to use it before attempting to sleep her.
