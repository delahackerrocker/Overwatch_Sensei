<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Since Echo is a flying character, you are one of the best possible counters. Your extreme range combined with high accuracy and reasonably high damage can make short work of Echo if she floats out in the open. An aware Echo would be harder to hit, especially with Sleep Dart. Be careful if she tried to flank as both Sticky Bombs and Focusing Beam can shred through your health quickly.

While copying you might lower her survivability (since you have no movement abilities), she can still charge up Nano-Boost quickly and possibly empower one of her allies, especially a tank.
