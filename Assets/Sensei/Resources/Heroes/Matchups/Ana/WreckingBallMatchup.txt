<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Wrecking Ball's combos can be extremely lethal if you are caught unaware, and he is nearly impossible for you to kill on your own due to his high mobility and health. However, his often predictable movement and large hitbox shouldn't make him too hard to sleep. If you are caught in his Minefield, you can use Biotic Grenade to quickly destroy a small portion of the mines to give yourself some breathing room, otherwise the mines can be destroyed with a single shot.
