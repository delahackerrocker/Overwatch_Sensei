<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Winston's mobility will let him easily chase you down if you try to flee from him. Although you can use Blink or Recall to temporarily avoid him, if Winston is out to get you, he can just keep using Jump Pack to get to your position. Your effective range is almost the same range as Winston's Tesla Cannon, and he can dance around his Barrier Projector to keep himself safe. However, if you stand your ground and attack back, you can tear through his large health while Blinking into his blind spots and using Recall to heal if you need to. If you think you can't handle Winston on your own, though, try to regroup with your team; while he can handle you on your own if you run, he'll have a harder time handling you with your teammates backing you up.
