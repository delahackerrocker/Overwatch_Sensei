<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While you lack Hanzo's ability to ascend walls, you can usually reach his position by traveling through flanking side routes. If you get Hanzo into your range, you'll have the upper hand so long as you dodge his arrows with Blink. If Hanzo activates Storm Arrows, consider hiding under cover until his duration wears out; while Blink can help you easily avoid individual arrows, dodging a burst of arrows will prove to be difficult, and it will only take one or two to kill you. Other than that, don't let Hanzo get distance on you, but rather put pressure on him and stay as close as you can.
