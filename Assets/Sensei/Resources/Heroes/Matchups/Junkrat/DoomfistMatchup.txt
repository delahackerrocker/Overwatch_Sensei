<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Don’t let Doomfist ever get the drop on you, a fully charged rocket punch is capable of killing you, and his combos, while they might not necessarily kill you, you’re still losing most of your Heath should you survive, if there’s a doomfist on the other team be wary of where he could try to flank you and either place a concussion mine and create distance or put a steel trap in-front of you to deny his rocket punch and slam and trap him, in which case he’s easy pickings.
