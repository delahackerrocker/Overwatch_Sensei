<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Unless you're caught by surprise or you're at low health, Brigitte shouldn't cause much trouble, Both of you need to be in close range to deal effective damage, and while she has her whip shot, it isn't enough to kill you directly if you are above half health, If you see her approaching directly at you, place a steel trap directly infront of you to try to trap her should she use shield bash, in which case she's easy enough to throw grenades at, If the fight is turning against your favor place a concussion mine between you two to create distance, to which she has no means to catch up to you unless she's using Rally or try to use whip shot as a last resort to finish you off.
