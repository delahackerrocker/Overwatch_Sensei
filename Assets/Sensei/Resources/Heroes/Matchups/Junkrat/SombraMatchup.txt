<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While Sombra's Hack will stop you from throwing Concussion Mine and Steel Trap, your grenades can still make quick work of her. She has no means of protection other than her Translocator. Even if she's in Stealth, she will be trapped if she steps on your Steel Trap, and she will be incapable of using Translocator. Bottleneck hallways with your Steel Trap and kill her as soon as she steps in it. If Sombra lands a hack on you as you're activating RIP-Tire you will lose your Ultimate and all of its charge; while it will be hard for her to time this, activate it when she isn't nearby just to be safe.
