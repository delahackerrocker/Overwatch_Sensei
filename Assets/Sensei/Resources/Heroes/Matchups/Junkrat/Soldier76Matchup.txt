<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Soldier: 76 can shoot you from long range with ease, and his Helix Rockets at close range can finish you off or destroy your RIP-Tire. However, he's much less dangerous at close range; a combination of Frag Launcher shots and your Concussion Mine can usually kill him quickly in a burst of damage. If Soldier: 76 flees with Sprint, consider whether to chase him down with your Concussion Mine or if it's better to hang back in case his teammates are nearby.
