<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Due to Ashe being a sniper, she’s an effective counter to you, She’ll usually stay out of your effective range and shoot at you freely, Don’t try to close the distance with your concussion mines since she can easily shoot you while you’re approaching and create distance with her coach gun, the most effective way to deal with Ashe is to stay out of her sights and flank her: a grenade + concussion mine combo will kill her.
