<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Pharah is nearly impossible to hit with your Frag Launcher while she is in the air, and the splash damage from her rockets will regularly destroy both Concussion Mine and Steel Trap. She can avoid RIP-Tire easily, and even kill it before it has a chance to get to her team. Steer clear of her if you can, and try to take fights into close quarters where you're more comfortable.
