<b>MATCH-UP</b>
There are no fancy tips for dealing with Cassidy as Ashe, it really just comes down to the range of the duel. Up close, Cassidy will almost always win with his Flashbang-Fan the Hammer combo and slightly higher health. However, at longer distances Ashe has the advantage because Cassidy's Peacekeeper will start to fall off while your scoped shots will not. Just don't take the fight too far or your gun will start to fall off too and while his damage will be significantly weaker, neither of you will be doing enough to actually kill one another. To put it simply, only take mid range duels with Cassidy because you'll have a natural advantage.
