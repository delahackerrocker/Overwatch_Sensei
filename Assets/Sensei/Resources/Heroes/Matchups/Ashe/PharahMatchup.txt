<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Because your scoped shots are hitscan, you counter Pharah while she's in the air, so if she's ever in rage, it's as simple as gunning her out of the sky. If you confident in your skills with dynamite, you can throw one at her and detonate it mid-air for additional pressure. If Pharah tries to flank you, can easily react with Coach Gun because her jetpacks are pretty loud, and remember to Coach Gun back onto the map if she boops you off. She's also very easy to kill while using Barrage as long as she's not firing rockets at you.
