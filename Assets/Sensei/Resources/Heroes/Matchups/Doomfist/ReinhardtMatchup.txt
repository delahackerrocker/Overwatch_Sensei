<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Reinhardt’s Steadfast passive ability makes him challenging to “toss around” like Doomfist can do to many other Heroes. Along with this, Reinhardt’s high health pool and high close-range damage makes him a risky target to focus without extensive help from your team. Rocket Punch can be used to force Reinhardt’s shield down for a split-second and Rising Uppercut can be used to launch Reinhardt in the air, leaving his team exposed for a very short period of time.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Reinhardt and Doomfist work very well together due to the fact they must both engage at a very close range (brawl). Having Reinhardt nearby allows Doomfist to focus more on dealing damage and less on escaping, as Reinhardt is able to use his Barrier Shield or take focus off of Doomfist.
