<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Mercy is a difficult target to pin down, due to Guardian Angel. Valkyrie only amplifies this, making her movement similar to Pharahs. A counter-play against Mercy is to keep an eye on enemy corpses, as she'll want to Resurrect one. This is a perfect opportunity to Rocket Punch to stun her out of her Resurrect or eliminate her entirely if you're lucky.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You can protect a Mercy Resurrecting a fallen ally with your abilities. Otherwise, a friendly Mercy using Valkyrie can increase the damage taken from you when you engage and keep you alive.
