<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Be careful of Ana's Sleep Dart, as it will most likely get you eliminated if it connects. Catching Ana unaware or catching her without her Sleep Dart is the best way to engage on her.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Ana works well with Doomfist, a Sleep Dart can most likely guarantee an elimination with Rocket Punch if the enemy is not a Tank. Biotic Grenade renders enemies unable to receive healing if you engage them. Nano Boost can make Doomfist even more deadly if you attack multiple targets. Nano Boost also increases damage taken from Meteor Strike.
