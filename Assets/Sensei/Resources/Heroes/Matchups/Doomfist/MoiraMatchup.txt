<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Watch for Moira to use her Fade, as she can use it to escape your abilities. Although difficult, you can follow Moira after she uses Fade with your Rocket Punch, though this is not recommended. You can cancel Moira's Coalescence with Rocket Punch though this is harder to do with her increased movement speed.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Coalescence can help keep you alive while engaging the enemy team. Be careful, as it won't protect you from burst damage such as Tracer's Pulse Bomb.
