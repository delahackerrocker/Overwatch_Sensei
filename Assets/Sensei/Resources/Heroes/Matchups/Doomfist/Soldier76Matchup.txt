<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Soldier 76 is a very easy match-up for Doomfist, while at range he clearly has you beaten, at close distance you can quite easily beat him with your abilities and knock him out of his Biotic Field should he deploy it, as long as you don’t run at him from a distance (in which case, he can easily kill you) or face him with low health, you should win.
