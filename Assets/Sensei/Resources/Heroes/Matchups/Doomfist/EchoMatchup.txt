<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You can’t do much about an enemy Echo, most of the time she’ll be out of your range due to her tendency of being airborne and she’ll usually land most of her abilities on you due to her mobility, the only chance you can potentially kill an Echo is with a Rocket Punch while she’s unaware, and it must hit a wall, should Echo use her Ultimate on you be wary of Meteor Strike and always save your Rocket Punch so you have a reliable escape tool.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Other than capitalizing on a friendly Echo's Sticky Bombs, there is no known synergy between Echo and Doomfist.
