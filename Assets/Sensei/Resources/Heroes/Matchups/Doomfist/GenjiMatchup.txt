<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Genji cannot deflect any of your abilities, and due to his relatively low health you shouldn’t have trouble with him other than him maybe pestering you at a distance with his primary fire, In Dragonblade, his Swift Strike cooldown is much lower, giving him an escape tool should the fight turn sour.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You and Genji diving into the enemy team is a good idea, similar to Winston. You both have escape options of things go wrong, and can take focus off of each other.
