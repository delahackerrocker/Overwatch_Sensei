<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Winston's value can be lowered by Doomfist significantly. The Best Defense... can keep you alive in a 1v1 due to Winston's low damage output. Rocket Punch can also give your team an opportunity to eliminate Winston by getting a head start in damage on him before he can escape. You can also force Winston away if he's going after a teammate with Primal Rage.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Winston and Doomfist diving into the enemy team is a good idea, Winston's damage can't be blocked, so he can soften enemies up in order for you to finish them off.
