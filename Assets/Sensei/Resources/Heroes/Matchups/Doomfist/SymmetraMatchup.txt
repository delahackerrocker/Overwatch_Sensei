<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Symmetra loses to Doomfist most of the time, as she has no movement abilities save for her Teleporter. Be wary of fighting Symmetra near her Sentry Turrets because they will slow you down a great deal. Be careful if her Photon Projector beam is fully charged. Also be careful of engaging an enemy while Photon Barrier is active as it will block your attacks.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Symmetra's Teleporter can allow for sneaky Rocket Punches or combo's.
