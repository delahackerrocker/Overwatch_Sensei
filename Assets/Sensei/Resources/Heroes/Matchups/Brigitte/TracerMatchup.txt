<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Due to her low health at 150HP, Tracer is one of Brigitte's easiest targets. However, her Pulse Pistols deal a lot of damage when close to her, and your best bet to stay alive is to give yourself and allies armor via Rally or a Repair Pack.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
With Brigitte's Repair Pack or Rally abilities, she can give Tracer more armor to sustain damage. One armor pack increases Tracer's health up to 200HP, making you her primary healer to depend on.
