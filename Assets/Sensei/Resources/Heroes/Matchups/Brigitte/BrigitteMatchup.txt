<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If you're up against another Brigitte, never Shield Bash her first, as you're vulnerable to other attacks from members of the other team ganging up on you. Apart from that, ensure to keep attacking her with your Whip Shot and Rocket Flail instead to take her out in a 1v1.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Brigitte has no notable team synergy with another Brigitte in No Limits.
