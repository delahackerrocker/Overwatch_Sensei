<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Roadhog can pull you and your allies out of Immortality Field. If predicted, you can dodge his hook with Exo-Boots and call it out to your team. Again, if predicted, toss Immortality Field at the enemy Roadhog so that if he hooks you, he cannot kill you.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Scrap Gun can deal up to 300 damage per shot with Baptiste's ultimate ability. Amplification Matrix and Whole Hog make a high damage combo. In the event where Roadhog gets hit by Biotic Grenade or Sleep Dart, Baptiste can use Immorality Field to protect him. Baptiste synergizes well when playing bunker-style.
