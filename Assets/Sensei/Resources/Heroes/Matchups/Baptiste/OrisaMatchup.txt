<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Orisa tends to play fixed positions, and her shield can be destroyed quickly with Amplification Matrix or about eight bursts from your Biotic Launcher. Orisa can use Halt! to pull you out of corners or Immortality Field (if you are standing at the edge). Orisa can attack you at long range and tank some of your shots with Fortify. Orisa can also increase her damage with Supercharger.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Paring Baptiste up with Orisa gives the entire team good survivability, safe spots to fire from, and cover to fall back and receive healing. Pairing Orisa's Supercharger up with Baptiste's Amplification Matrix also gives the entire team a window of opportunity to deal an immense amount of damage to the enemy.
