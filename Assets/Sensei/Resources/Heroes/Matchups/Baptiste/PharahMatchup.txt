<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your Biotic Launcher's primary fire can be a useful tool to dispatch an enemy Pharah, however this should only be done at medium ranges, as your recoil and damage falloff will make killing her harder at longer ranges. Amplification Matrix can help you and any allied Pharah counters kill her much faster, while Immortality Field can be used to save your team from a Rocket Barrage. Try and throw the Field as far as you can from her ultimate splash radius, as it can be quickly destroyed by Rocket Barrage.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
When shot through Amplification Matrix, Pharah's rockets will deal twice the damage. Her Rocket Launcher can quickly kill enemies, and Rocket Barrage can shred through entire teams.
