<b>MATCH-UP</b>
A Hack from Sombra can keep you on ground without Wraith Form to flee from or pursue her and her team. Her Hack and EMP will also stop Death Blossom mid-use, blinding your action in the team fight. However, you have more firepower to use against Sombra and a large amount of health. Even if hacked, in a 1 on 1 situation you should be able to handle Sombra at close range.
