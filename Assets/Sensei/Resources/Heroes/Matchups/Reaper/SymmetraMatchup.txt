<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Symmetra Photon Projector has high damage output just like your Hellfire Shotguns, but her health is 200 compared to your 250, and at point blank range you should be able to outdamage her even if her weapon is powered up. Should you spot her turrets, a single shot should be able to take them out. If caught off guard by multiple turrets, though, it may be wiser to use Wraith Form to fall back and heal before going back in to take the turrets out. A well-timed Photon Barrier can block all of the damage from Death Blossom, so be wary.
