<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Baptiste's Exo Boots and Immortality Field allow him to escape you easily to a range where he's more comfortable. His Immortality Field will also completely neuter your Ultimate and save his team from it. Either destroy or wait it out before you death blossom.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
If you feel the need for additional security when using Death Blossom, you can request a friendly Baptiste to throw his Immortality Field your way. This will keep you safe for the duration of your Ultimate, and can help rip through hunkered down enemies that would normally kill you before you can kill them, such as a Bastion set up with an Orisa and Baptiste.
