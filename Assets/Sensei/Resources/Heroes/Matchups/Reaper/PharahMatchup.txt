<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Pharah is a strong counter to Reaper because she will usually be hovering in the air where you can't do anything to her. She can also spam you down from afar with rockets and displace you with Concussive Blast, forcing you to Wraith Form every time. If you don't have a ranged hero suitable for dealing with Pharah, it's advised you switch to one if she is a problem.
