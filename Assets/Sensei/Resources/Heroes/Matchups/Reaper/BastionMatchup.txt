<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Bastion will tear you down in seconds if he catches you out in the open, therefore he can be quite problematic for Reaper. If he's alone and not being supported by his team, you can attempt to flank him and take him out by yourself, however you will need to be quick and effective before he can react, as he can still out-damage you at your effective range if you're not careful.
