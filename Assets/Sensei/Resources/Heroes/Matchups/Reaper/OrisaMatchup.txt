<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Reaper is strong against Orisa, because her large hitbox and slow movement make her a perfect target to tear apart with your shotguns. Even through Fortify, your shotguns can make quick work of her in a 1v1 and your life steal can help you take any damage she does back to you. Due to Reaper's flanky playstyle and powerful escape tools, you can shut down her Supercharger before her team can use it and use your Wraith Form to flee. Don't be afraid of using Death Blossom to take her Ultimate out, as hers is significantly more valuable than yours.
