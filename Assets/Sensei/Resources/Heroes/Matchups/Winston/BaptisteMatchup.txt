<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Baptiste should not to be underestimated, he can lay down heavy damage output if he can land his shots, especially if he can land headshots on you, he can also easily gain high ground and regenerate his health with his abilities and his immortality field can give him enough time to finish you off if you’re not careful, if he is healing someone you’re currently attacking, it’ll be tough to actually do significant damage to your current target, however, when he deploys his immortality field try to sneak up to it, most people usually tend to stick close to the immortality field, and since your Tesla cannon can target multiple people at once, you can take this opportunity to gain ultimate charge, as well as dealing some damage to several people at once.
