<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Much like Phara, there’s very little you can do to take Echo down, most of the time she’ll be out of your effective range, and when she IS near you where you can deal consistent damage, she can usually just use flight and flee without much trouble, using your jump pack to damage her while she’s airborne will do very little, so don’t bother unless you’re certain she’s near death, The most you can do is deploy your barrier, which will not last long against her abilities, if there’s an enemy echo, do not bother and leave her to your team.
