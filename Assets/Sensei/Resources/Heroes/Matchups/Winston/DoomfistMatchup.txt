<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your Tesla Cannon is too weak to have an effect on Doomfist, especially because he can gain shields. Try to bait his Rocket Punch and use Jump Pack to dodge it, making him easier for you and your team to defeat. Try to avoid close-range combat by using Jump Pack to escape, and if he uses Meteor Strike, place a barrier at his landing location and attack him with your team from outside the barrier.
