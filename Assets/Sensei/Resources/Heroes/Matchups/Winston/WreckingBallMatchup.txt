<b>MATCH-UP</b>
The only advantage you have over Wrecking ball is that you can easily clear his minefield with your Tesla cannon, since it targets multiple targets and the mines have low health, but otherwise Wrecking ball will hold an advantage over you in a direct fight, his Massive health makes your Tesla cannon a pathetic weapon against his quad cannons, should you retreat he can catch up to you easily in his ball form, Which he can also use to retreat easily, if you’re encountered by a Wrecking ball try to stick with teammates that can handle him better rather than fighting him on your own.
