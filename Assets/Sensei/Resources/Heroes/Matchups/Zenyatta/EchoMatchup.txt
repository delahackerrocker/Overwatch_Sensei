<b>MATCH-UP</b>
When it comes to facing an enemy Echo as Zenyatta, You're going to need quick reflexes and good aiming, most of the time, an enemy Echo will consider you easy prey due to your lack of mobility and slow movement speed, which makes it easy for her to land her abilities, while this is true, you can usually defend yourself fairly well against an enemy Echo if you have good aim and she doesn't catch you by surprise, orb of discord also helps with this, if there's an enemy echo on the other team, keep watching your surroundings since an enemy Echo can easily flank you and your teammates due to her superior mobility, An enemy Echo duplicating you is rare, since there are usually better heroes to transform into, but should it happen, you should try to avoid facing her unless you're VERY confident with your aim, because even if you do win the matchup, once she loses all of her health during her ultimate she'll go back to being Echo, which can be very bad if you find yourself at low health
