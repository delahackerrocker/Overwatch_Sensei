<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Always be wary of Roadhog and always keep your distance from him. If you're too far away for him to snag you with his Chain Hook, he's a prime target for Orb of Discord and can be mowed down in seconds. If he sneaks up on you, though, one good Hook and you're down for the count. Stick with your team and be aware of his location whenever possible.
