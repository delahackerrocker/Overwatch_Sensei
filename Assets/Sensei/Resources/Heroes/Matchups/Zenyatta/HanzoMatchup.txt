<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You excel at fighting foes at long range; unfortunately, so does Hanzo. Your slow speed and predictable movement makes it easy for him to land shots on you. Even if you duck for cover indoors, Sonic Arrow will keep him notified of your exact position. If he manages to close the gap on you, Storm Arrows will quickly kill you before you have a chance to retaliate. Dragonstrike can also be very difficult to avoid; while Transcendence can help you escape the attack and protect your teammates, there are usually other Ultimate abilities that are more dangerous that you would want to save Transcendence for.
