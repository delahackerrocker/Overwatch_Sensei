<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Should Sombra hack you, you'll lose the ability to fire your Orbs of Harmony and Discord. If they're on a target prior to the hack, however, they will remain there until automatically recalled (via line of sight or if their target dies). Even without your Orbs, you can handle decently in a fight with Sombra. Just keep in mind that she can escape from a poor engagement with her Translocator. If she does so, it's usually not wise to pursue; your low mobility means it'll be difficult to back out if she lures you towards the rest of her team. EMP will immediately rob you of 75% of your health while also cutting off your ability to protect your team from a big push with Transcendence; if hit by EMP, huddle behind your team and pray they can protect you, because with no mobility or health, you're going to be a prime target.
