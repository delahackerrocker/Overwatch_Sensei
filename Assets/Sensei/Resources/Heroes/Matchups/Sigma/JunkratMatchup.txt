<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Junkrat's heavy spam damage will quickly destroy your Experimental Barrier. Close the gap with Kinetic Grasp, as the high damage output of Junkrat's explosives will give you plenty of extra shield health to work with. You can also destroy RIP-Tire with a single burst of your Hyperspheres.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
An enemy team lifted into the air with Gravitic Flux serves as an easy target for a friendly Junkrat to blow apart with RIP-Tire.
