<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your Hyperspheres can quickly break Brigitte's weak barrier, and your Accretion can knock down any over-zealous Brigittes. Your Experimental Barrier and Kinetic Grasp will do nothing against her melee primary, but Experimental Barrier will block Whip Shot, so try and block as many Whip Shots as possible to deny her Inspire healing.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You and Brigitte lack mobility and range; if you stick together, though, you can keep each other and your team alive and allow your teammates to wreak havoc.
