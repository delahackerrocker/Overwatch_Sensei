<b>MATCH-UP</b>


<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Experimental Barrier's mobility can provide good cover for a friendly Cassidy to get up close and damage enemies while giving him ample protection. If you lift the enemy team into the air with Gravitic Flux, Cassidy can easily gun them down with Deadeye. Just be careful about a lifted Reinhardt, as he'll still be able to deploy his barrier to protect any teammates floating behind him.
