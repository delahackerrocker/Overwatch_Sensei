<b>MATCH-UP</b>
While you are planning on using your ultimate ability, be very wary of Baptiste's signature ability, immortality field, as it is a very hard counter to your ultimate. Baptiste's Amplification Matrix will amplify the damage ouput of any projectiles that pass through, so absorbing the damage with Kinetic Grasp is a good idea, as you will be able to gain a massive amount of bonus shields.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Sigma has no notable team synergy with Baptiste.
