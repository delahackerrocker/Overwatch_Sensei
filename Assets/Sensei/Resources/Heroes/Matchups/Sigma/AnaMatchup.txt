<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You can place your Experimental Barrier between Ana and her team to stop her from being able to heal them. One of the most important things you should be doing is using Experimental Barrier to block her Biotic Grenade and Sleep Dart.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You're modestly powerful when Nano-Boosted by Ana; your high-powered Hyperpheres can plow through enemies, but their low range and your low mobility means that you should only be asking for Ana to hit you with her Nano-Boost in a dire situation where you need to defend an objective. Try to stick with Ana at most times; you both have low mobility, and you can protect each other from flankers.
