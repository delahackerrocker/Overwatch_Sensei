<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Mercy's high mobility and self-healing can make her difficult to shoot down. One of the most important things you can do against a Mercy is cancelling her Resurrection with your Accretion. If you know a Mercy has Resurrection available, save Accretion to stun her out of it, which should be easy to hit considering Resurrection has a slow cast time and Mercy is almost completely still while using it.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Mercy can boost all of your damage making you a powerful tank due to your increased damage.
