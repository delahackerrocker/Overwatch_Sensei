<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Both you and Junkrat are dangerous in close corridors. It's crucial to catch him by surprise with Flashbang, otherwise his grenades will make quick work of you. Even if you kill Junkrat in a close-quarters fight, Total Mayhem's bombs may end up killing you, as you have no means of escaping them other than Combat Roll. Watch your step, as getting caught in a Steel Trap will certainly spell your doom.
