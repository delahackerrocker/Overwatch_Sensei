<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Lúcio will usually be protected behind his teammates or riding over a high wall. To kill kill him from afar, you'll need to be extra careful with your shots due to his small hitbox and his health regeneration. His Amp It Up on speed boost can help his teammates retreat or advance on your position faster than you can respond. Be careful when you're perched on high ground, as Lúcio can disturb you with a knockback from his Soundwave. Should he attempt to approach you like this, though, he'll leave himself open to a Flashbang. Try not to use Deadeye right after Lúcio uses Sound Barrier, as his teammates will most likely survive your shots.
