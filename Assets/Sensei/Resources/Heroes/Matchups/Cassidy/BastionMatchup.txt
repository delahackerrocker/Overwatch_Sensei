<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You'll win most encounters with Bastion if you catch him in Recon Configuration. While he's in Sentry Configuration, though, it's an entirely different story. It will be difficult to shoot his weak spot on his rear unless you sneak up on him, and even then he'll most likely withstand your attacks, then swivel around and rip through you. Attacking him point blank is a dangerous tactic, as he can quickly gun you down. Should you peek in and out of barriers or cover, though, and he isn't likewise protected, you can land a few safe shots on Bastion and eventually kill him.
