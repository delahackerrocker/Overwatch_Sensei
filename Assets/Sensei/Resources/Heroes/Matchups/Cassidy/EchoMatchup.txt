<b>MATCH-UP</b>
As long as you can consistently hit your shots, you're one of Echo's harshest counters, you can gun her down in seconds with your peacekeeper even while airborne, and while it's highly unlikely you'll ever get to do a fan the hammer combo on her, you should only worry about her focusing beam if you're under half health or her using her ultimate on you, since she can build deadeye way faster than you can.
