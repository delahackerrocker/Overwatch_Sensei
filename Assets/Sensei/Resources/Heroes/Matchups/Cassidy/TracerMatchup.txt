<b>MATCH-UP</b>
With her constant mobility, Tracer will prove to be a massive pain to kill at a distance. If you land Flashbang on her, though, that's another story; a Flashbang followed by a single headshot will be sufficient to rip through her minimal health. Even without Flashbang, a single headshot will put her at 10 health, making her an easy target for your teammates to finish off so long as they're focused on her. Try to stay by your Support or Tank allies, as Tracer will probably try to flank and harass them; you're their best defense should you catch her by surprise with a Flashbang.
