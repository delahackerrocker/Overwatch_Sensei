<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If you don't let Doomfist sneak up on you with a rocket punch, you can handle pretty well against him, Flashbang can interrupt any of Doomfist's abilities, which can ruin any momentum and ruin any of his combos, fan the hammer can potentially kill Doomfist or leave him with low health making him easy to finish off, if you're using Deadeye be wary of him sneaking up on you since you move slower, making you an easy target if he's out of your sights.
