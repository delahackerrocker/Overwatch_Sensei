<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Baptiste can prove to be annoying to you, while in a one on one fight at close quarters you'll usually come on top, what really proves to be a problem is his immortality field, this can protect his teammates from your fan the hammer combo, and deadeye, since it only locks on enemy heroes, not objects, at a distance he also shouldn't be underestimated, since he can lay out a heavy damage output with his primary fire, his healing output is also pretty high if he knows how to utilize them which can be troublesome.
