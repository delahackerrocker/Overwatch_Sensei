<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Torbjörn himself is of little threat to you, but when he has Overload active, he'll be tougher for you to kill. Try to surprise him before he can use it; if you can't kill him in time, though, use Combat Roll to gain distance from him until Overload wears off or you've regrouped with your team. You can easily destroy Torbjörn's Turret by peeking in and out of cover while firing at it. Flashbang does effect the Turret, but you will usually have better targets to use it on. Your low mobility means it may be easy for Torbjörn to catch you in his Molten Core, so be careful.
