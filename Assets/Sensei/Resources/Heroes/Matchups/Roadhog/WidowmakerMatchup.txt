<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Widowmaker is capable of quickly taking you out before you can get anywhere near close enough to try to Chain Hook her. Even once you close in, Widowmaker can use her Grappling Hook to quickly get away. However, on Control and Assualt maps with largely closed-in control points, it can be extremely difficult for Widowmaker to dislodge you from the point. Keep out of Widowmaker's line of sight while making sure to hook her allies as they approach you.
