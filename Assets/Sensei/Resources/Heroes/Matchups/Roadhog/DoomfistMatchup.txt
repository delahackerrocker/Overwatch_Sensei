<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Doomfist shouldn't be an issue for you. While Rocket Punch can stun you and cancel your self-heal, he doesn't exactly have the combo potential to kill you. Plus, you can hook him and a headshot will finish him off. Even a direct hit from Meteor Strike only strips you of half of your health, which can he recovered with your self-heal. Doomfist might have some luck if can knock you into an environmental hazard.
