<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Moira doesn't pose a huge threat to you but she can avoid your attacks with Fade. Moira can also use her secondary fire to heal herself and replenish her resource meter. Since you're so huge, it won't be hard to lock a beam onto you. You can hook Moira out of Coalescence, cancelling the ult. Even at close range, she can still evade you with Fade (if timed correctly).
