<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Genji and Roadhog can have a difficult time killing one another. Genji can outrange Roadhog with his primary fire and use Deflect to block his Chain Hook. On the flip-side, Roadhog's massive health and self-healing makes trying to kill him impractical for Genji. If Genji survives being Chain Hooked and shot by you, it's likely that he'll either use Deflect to back away, dash away with Swift Strike, or both. Should he dash away, you may be able to use your alt-fire to finish Genji as he flees. This matchup is for the most part a stalemate depending on what cooldowns are available to Genji.
