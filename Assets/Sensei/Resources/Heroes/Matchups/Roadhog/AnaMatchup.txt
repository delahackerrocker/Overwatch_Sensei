<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Ana's Sleep Dart can save her from a flank or save an ally from your hook combo; likewise, it can shut down your Ultimate quite easily. Her Biotic Grenade shuts down your self-healing, too. However, Roadhog has far more health than she does, and deals a lot of damage; Ana will mostly want to Sleep Dart you and then flee, unless she has an ally nearby who can help finish you off. If you have to approach her in the open, she can deal quite a bit of damage to you from outside of your hook range. However, Ana will die to the hook combo, and she lacks any sort of mobility ability to escape or to make her harder to hit; if you manage to flank her, or simply get in close without being Sleep Darted, Ana will die.
