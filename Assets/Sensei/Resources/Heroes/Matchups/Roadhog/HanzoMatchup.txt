<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Hanzo often hangs back way beyond Roadhog's effective range. Short of a headshot, Roadhog can take Hanzo's arrows like a pincushion (sometimes literally with Storm Arrows). Once close, however, Hanzo has little defense against Roadhog short of well-placed headshots with his standard or Storm Arrows. How easy the fight will be entirely depends on how well the Hanzo can make headshots and how well you can avoid them.
