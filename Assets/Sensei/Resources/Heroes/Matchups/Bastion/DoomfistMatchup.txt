<b>MATCH-UP</b>


<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Throughout the duration of the match, Doomfist will be trying to go in deep, leaping into enemy ranks and wreaking as much havoc as he can. Because of his reliance on melee-oriented airstrike attacks, you should coordinate with a friendly Doomfist and cover his rush with your surveillance. Doomfist will be able to scare enemies out of cover, leaving them susceptible to your shots, and his spectacular mobility will more than likely be able to pick off foes that you weaken in Configuration: Sentry.
