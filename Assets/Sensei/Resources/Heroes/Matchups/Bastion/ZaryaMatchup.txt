<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your only real concern when fighting Zarya is to make sure you don't attack her or her allies while her Barrier is active. While her powered laser won't out-damage your gatling gun, if she's supported by teammates she can be kept alive long enough to kill you before you can kill her. If she approaches you with her Barrier up, it is actually sometimes more advantageous to attack; ripping through her Barrier will power her up, but it will also cause it to dissipate quicker, meaning if you're accurate you should still be able to kill her before she kills you.
