<b>MATCH-UP</b>
D.Va can use her Defense Matrix to completely block your attacks for a brief period, allowing her teammates to finish you off easily. If this happens, try to shoot in a direction that Defense Matrix isn't covering. It's rarely smart to try to retreat while Defense Matrix is active, as this will just make it easier for her teammates to kill you; Defense Matrix is brief, and once D.Va has used it up, it will be easy to shred through her and destroy her Mech. Be careful about D.Va's Self-Destruct; your low mobility will make it difficult to escape in time.
