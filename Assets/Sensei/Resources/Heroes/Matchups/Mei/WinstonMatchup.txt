<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Winston's Tesla Cannon will barely chip away at your higher than average health. While it's hard to escape from him, he'll have difficulty killing you if you use your abilities wisely. As long as you make sure he isn't protected by his Barrier Projector, his large size makes him an easy target for your Endothermic Blaster. Be careful of his Primal Rage Ultimate, though; if you're knocked off a cliff, you'll have no way to save yourself.
