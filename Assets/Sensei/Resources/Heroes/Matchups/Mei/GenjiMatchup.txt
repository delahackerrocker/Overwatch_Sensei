<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Genji's greatest strengths are his mobility and close range damage, both of which fall apart due to Mei's freezing ability at close range. While both your Icicle and Blizzard Ultimate can be Deflected, your Endothermic Blaster's primary fire and area of effect damage cannot, and your Ice Wall can be used to trap Genji in small rooms where he can't dodge effectively. Genji's only advantage is his ability to use Swift Strike or Cyber-Agility to quickly escape from Blizzard or being blocked in larger passages. If Genji is heckling you or your teammates, make him a priority to freeze and kill.
