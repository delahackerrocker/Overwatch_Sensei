<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Tracer's slim frame is hard to hit with arrows, especially at close range. Her Blink also makes her slippery at long range. With Sonic Arrow, though, you can keep tabs on where she's hiding, and you can prepare an arrow for when she peeks her head out from cover. If Tracer does try to flank you, use Storm Arrows to run through her before she can kill you, or use Wall Climb to get to a higher location where she can't chase you.
