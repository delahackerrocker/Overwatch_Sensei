<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Like Orisa, Reinhardt's barrier makes it difficult for Hanzo to get his damage off. In addition, should Reinhardt somehow close the distance, Hanzo will be easily dispatched in just a couple of swings. However, once his barrier goes down, Reinhardt can be easily shot down in a few moments.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Earthshatter and Dragonstrike make for a powerful combo, as the long stun duration means the opponent likely won't be able to get out of the way before the dragons hit them.
