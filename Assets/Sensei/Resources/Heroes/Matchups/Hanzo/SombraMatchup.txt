<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Your high burst makes you an imminent danger to Sombra at all times, and your Sonic Arrow can reveal her for your team and foil her element of surprise. As such, a good Sombra will try to make you one of her priority targets. Keep Sonic Arrow up as often as possible to make it difficult for her to sneak up easily, and if you get hacked, stand your ground: one good shot can either end the fight or force her to retreat.
