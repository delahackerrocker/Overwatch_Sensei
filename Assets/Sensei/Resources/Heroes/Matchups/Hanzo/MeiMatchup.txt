<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Always maintain your distance from Mei: you stand no chance against her at close range by yourself. Her Ice Wall can block your arrows for a short period of time, and her Cryo-Freeze can make it difficult to burst her down with anything that isn't a fully-charged headshot. Overall, just keep your distance and don't hesitate to run if she hits you with an icicle.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Thanks to her strong CC capabilities, Mei can combo up well with Hanzo's massive concentrated burst. Like Earthshatter and Graviton Surge, Mei's Blizzard can set up an enemy team for a Dragonstrike for massive damage.
