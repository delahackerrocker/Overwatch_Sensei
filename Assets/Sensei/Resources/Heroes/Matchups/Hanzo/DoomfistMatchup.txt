<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You have advantages over Doomfist, at range, you can easily shoot at him with barely any means for Doomfist to retaliate and be left exposed without abilities, Sonic arrow can be used to more easily track him, and if he manages to get close to you your storm arrows can deal devastating damage, if not outright kill him, don’t get too cocky though, if you’re missing your shots or the enemy Doomfist is flanking you, he can usually kill you before you can kill him.
