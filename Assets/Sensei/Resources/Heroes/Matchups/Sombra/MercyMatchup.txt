<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If you land a hack on Mercy, she loses Guardian Angel, her prime method of escape. Without it, she'll be incredibly easy to gun down if her team isn't nearby to protect her. If a Mercy is in the process of using Resurrect, hacking her will cut it off and provide you and your team enough time to finish her off.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Sombra has no notable team synergy with Mercy.
