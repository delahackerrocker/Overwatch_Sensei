<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Defense Matrix negates very few of Doomfist's abilities, and his consistent stunning moves can make escaping him excessively difficult. Choosing to fight him head-on alone will also end badly, as his ability to generate shields means he'll always be able to outlast you until your mech is destroyed, at which point you're as good as gone. Keep your distance, and only engage when you have teammates to help you.
