<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Both you and Reaper need close range to effectively deal damage, and at close range Reaper will rip through you in seconds. While negating his Ability-reaper4.png Death Blossom Ultimate is very helpful for your team, in most other situations your only real options when fighting Reaper are to negate his attacks or flee, but even then Ability-reaper3.png Shadow Step and Ability-reaper2.png Wraith Form can help him chase you down and finish you off.
