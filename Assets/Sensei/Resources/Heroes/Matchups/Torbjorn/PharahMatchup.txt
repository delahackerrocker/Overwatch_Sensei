<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Pharah can very easily deal with your Turret, dealing max damage direct hits onto it as much as she likes. However, in a defensive setup with proper protection, your Turret serves as an excellent area denial that is a great nuisance to Pharah. Set up your turret where it can cover the most airspace, and be careful when she ducks behind cover to engage the Turret. This can be especially helpful if you're protected by a Reinhardt or Orisa's barrier; they can soak up Pharah's negligible damage, and your Turret can shoot her down and keep her out of the air.
