<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Roadhog can take a lot of your Turret's firepower and still survive, but his Scrap Gun can't deal much damage to it from a distance and his Chain Hook cannot grab it. He'll most likely try to get behind your defenses to hook you away from the Turret and kill you so the Turret is easy pickings for him and his team. However, if you manage to keep your distance, him soaking up damage can rapidly fill your Ultimate ability, and his slow mobility means Molten Core can hit him for a great deal of damage. Overload is a useful tool against his Scrap Gun, potentially even helping you survive Roadhog's Hook combo.
