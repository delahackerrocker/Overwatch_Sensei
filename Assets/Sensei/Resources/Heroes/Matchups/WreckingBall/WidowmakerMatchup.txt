<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Wrecking Ball is probably one of her biggest counters. As long as he's in Roll form, she can't land critical hits on him. Wrecking Ball can also pursue Widowmaker, no matter where she goes, with enough damage to kill her. Adaptive Shield can also be used to soak up some of her damage. At close range, Wrecking Ball can shred through her health with Quad Cannon and a Quick Melee.
