<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Zenyatta should be easy to deal with at close range. However, at a distance, it could be different. Orbs of Destruction, his weapon, has no damage falloff, and a full volley could deal up to 240 HP of damage. This can be further compounded with Orb of Discord, increasing the damage you take from anything. With Transcendence, Zenyatta can simply charge through your mines without taking damage and heal heavily wound allies.
