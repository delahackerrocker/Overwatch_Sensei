<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Ana's Sleep Dart is a danger to Wrecking Ball as it both stops his momentum and force him out of Roll. This can give her team ample time to lay down focus fire and potentially kill you.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Ana can heal Wrecking Ball at a distance with scoped shots and use a Biotic Grenade to increase the healing output. Nano Boost applied to Wrecking Ball makes him harder to kill, and that is compounded further when he uses Adaptive Shields.
