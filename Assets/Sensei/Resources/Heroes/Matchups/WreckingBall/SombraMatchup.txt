<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Much like Mei, Sombra's Hack can easily shut you down. It forces you out of Roll, you can't use Grappling Claw, and there's almost a guarantee that you'll die if you're hacked in the middle of a team fight.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
EMP can disable enemies with movement abilities, which means they won't be able to avoid your Minefield as you deploy it.
