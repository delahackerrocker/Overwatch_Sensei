<b>MATCH-UP</b>
If you and D.Va both stay put and fire away at one another, you're almost certain to lose this matchup. However, due to your high mobility, this will rarely be the case. While it's unlikely that you will ever land a kill on her without support from your allies, your primary asset against her is being a nuisance who will refuse to die. Getting rammed by Boosters can temporarily disrupt your swinging, but even then you can simply roll away from a fight if you don't wish to continue.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
If your team decides to run both D.Va and Wrecking Ball as your tanks, you're offering your team extremely minimal protection. That being said, if your team is filled with heroes who prefer to stay mobile and fend for themselves, such as Genji, Tracer, and Lucio, this may be an acceptable, aggressive team composition.
