<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Wrecking Ball's enormous health total makes the Tesla Cannon do little more than tickle him, and you have better mobility than him. Also, he has less health than you, so you should be able to kill with Quad Cannons at close range.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
You and Winston are one of the most mobile tanks, and one of you has a shield. When your shield starts to run low, he can deploy his Barrier Projector to act as a secondary line of defense. Primal Rage can be used to knock enemies into your Minefield.
