<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Shield Bash is one of the most dangerous abilities to Wrecking Ball. Similar to Flash Bang, t's fast enough to catch you during a rollout, cut off your Ultimate, and make you vulnerable to his team. Make sure you're watching Brigitte's ability usage so you know when it's safe to engage. Also, since you're a large target, Brigitte can easily hit you with her flail and activate Inspire.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Brigitte can heal him at range with Repair Packs. Rally armor can also strengthen his defense and increase his vitality as he dives into the enemy team.
