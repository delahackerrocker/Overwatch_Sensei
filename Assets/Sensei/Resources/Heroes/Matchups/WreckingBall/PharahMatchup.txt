<b>MATCH-UP</b>
You're at a disadvantage against Pharah. As long as she in the sky, she's essentially out of your reach as you have no means to get to her. Quad Cannon won't do enough damage to kill her quickly. Plus, she can fire rockets at you, even as you try to flee. Be wary of Barrage, because it can chip away both your health and Adaptive Shields (if you have it active).

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
There's no known synergy between her and Wrecking Ball
