<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Mei's Endothermic Blaster almost entirely shuts you down, and Ice Wall can easily halt you. Most of the time, it's better to simply go to your spawn room and switch to a different hero instead of throwing yourself at her over and over again.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Enemies frozen by Blizzard are the prefect targets for Minefield. Just drop your mines from above and watch them get blown to bits.
