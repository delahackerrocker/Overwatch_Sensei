<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Moira is difficult to deal with, she has a small hitbox and high survivability. She gives very little charge and her orbs don't give you any charge at all. Her ultimate does give some decent charge, but will also damage you heavily. But if you catch her without any of her abilities, you can melt her like a candle.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Moira can keep you alive as well as combo her Coalesence with your Graviton Surge from some easy kills. This is because her ulitmate pierces barriers (Except for Zarya's.)
