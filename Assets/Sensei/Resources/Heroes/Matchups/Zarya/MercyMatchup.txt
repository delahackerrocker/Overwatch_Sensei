<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Although Mercy has Guardian Angel to flee from most situations, it usually won't be far enough to send her out of your attack range. At low Energy, you may have difficulty finishing Mercy off, but at high Energy you should be able to rip through her before she can escape or heal herself. Mercy will usually stay behind her ally, so try not to engage in a 2-on-1 fight, as you will almost certainly lose.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Mercy is a good pocket for you, her damage boost can make you even stronger and her resurrection can bring you back into the fight. (Though your charge will remain lost.) Make sure to protect her with a barrier if she is using resurrection.
