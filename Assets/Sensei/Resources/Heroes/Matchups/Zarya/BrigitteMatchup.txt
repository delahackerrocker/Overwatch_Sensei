<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Brigette isn't much of a threat to you, just make sure to burn through her shield and try to avoid her shield bash. Beware though, your barrier will not protect you from her Whip-Flail's knockback.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Brigette plays very similarly to Reinhart, so the tips for him should apply to her as well.

Her ulitmate can make you even stronger, so stick close to her.
