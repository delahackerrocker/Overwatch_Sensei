<b>MATCH-UP</b>
Ashe will most likely be sniping you from a distance and away from your Particle Cannon's effective range. If she throws Dynamite at you, you can use your barriers to gain energy from it, especially from its burn overtime.

<b>TEAM SYNERGY</b>
Ashe is usually in the backlines of a match so you won't often meet up. But Ashe's Dynamite and Bob can be devastating if throw into a Graviton Surge.
