<b>STRENGTH</b>
Symmetra's abilities provide both defensive and positional advantages to her team. She can cover chokepoints with her Sentry Turrents, deploy a Teleporter to provide teammates higher ground or ambush the enemy team, and split the map in half with her Ultimate. Since most of her health is shields she can duck away from a fight for a few seconds and be back up to full health quickly. Her primary fire grows in power the longer she is damaging someone, this is especially true of tanks with shields. She eats shields for breakfast. Her secondary fire does explosive damage and is great for taking out stationary targets like turrets. Heroes abilities, like DVa's Self Destruct or Symmetra's own turrets can travel through her Teleporter.

<b>WEAKNESS</b>
Her low health and mobility means that she must coordinate with her team in order to survive. She's pretty useless against hitscan heroes at range since her secondary fire has a slow travel time. Landing a secondary fire against flying heroes like Pharah, Echo, and Mercy is near impossible. Her turrets can be destroyed by movement abilities like Wrecking Ball's Ball ability, Reinhardt's Charge, or Doomfist's Rocket Punch.
