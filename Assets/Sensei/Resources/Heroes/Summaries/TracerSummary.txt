<b>STRENGTH</b>
Tracer is a close-range skirmishing hero who specializes in speed and single-target damage. Her mobility and high single-target damage makes her excellent at picking key targets with very low risk. Tracer can quickly traverse a map with the use of Blink to outflank her enemies, deal damage to them, and then retreat before they have a chance to respond. 

<b>WEAKNESS</b>
Her low base Health and lack of regenerating shields means that she must make good use of Blink and Recall to avoid or reverse damage.
