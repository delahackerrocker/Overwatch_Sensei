<b>STRENGTH</b>
Baptiste's primary fire is a midrange sub-machine gun that fires in three-round bursts. His alternate fire launches healing grenades at mid range. His primary fire and secondary fire have thier own ammo but both are reloaded at the same time, he can also shoot both at the same time. While engaging the enemy, he can charge his Exo-Boots by crouching, which he can use to jump to high ground or escape attacks. Using Regenerative Burst at the start of an engagement can help your teammates tank sustained damage long enough to win the fight. You can use the Immortality Field to body block a door or a spawn room door. Throw the generator in a corner where it cannot be destroyed, then use Baptiste's body to prevent the enemy from coming through. Amplification Matrix doubles the damage of any projectiles or abilities that travel through it and can be used to decisively win team fights.

<b>WEAKNESS</b>
The healing grenades you fire cannot heal you personally, so just focus on your teammates' survival when flinging these around. If you need help yourself, you need to rely on another team member or your Regenerative Burst ability. Other than Health Packs, Regenerative Burst is Baptiste's only means of healing himself. Try not to get too near your enemies while the ability is on cooldown. Immortality Field has a long cooldown, it's best reserved for a powerful ult combo play made by the other team if you think one is close to dropping.
